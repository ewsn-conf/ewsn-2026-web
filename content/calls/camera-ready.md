All publications in the **EWSN'26** proceedings must adhere to the following formatting and submission guidelines. **Please note: The camera-ready formatting requirements differ from the initial submission format.**

### Mandatory Formatting Change

To ensure a consistent look across the proceedings and to provide more space for addressing reviewer feedback, **all papers must use a 9pt font**. This is a mandatory requirement for the final version.

- **Font Size: 9pt**
- **Layout**: Two-column format on 8.5″ x 11″ (Letter) paper.
- **Text Block**: Maximum 7″ wide x 9″ deep with an inter-column spacing of 0.25″.

### Content Requirements

- **CCS Concepts and Keywords**: These sections are **mandatory** and must be accurately selected to describe your work.
- **ACM Reference Format and Copyright block**: These sections are **not required** for EWSN'26 and **must be omitted**.

### LaTeX Configuration

Authors are required to use the following LaTeX commands in the document preamble to ensure uniformity:

```latex
\documentclass[sigconf, 9pt, nonacm]{acmart}
\settopmatter{printacmref=false}
```

---

### Submission Guidelines

**Page Limits**:
- **Full Papers**: Max 12 pages.
- **Short Papers**: Max 6 pages.
- _Note: These limits include all references, tables, and appendices._

**Short Paper Titles**:

The final camera-ready PDF of short papers must be prepended with one of the following, as applicable:
- "Vision Paper: [Title]"
- "Position Paper: [Title]"
- "WiP Paper: [Title]"

**LaTeX Sources**:

A *.zip archive containing all sources required to compile the final document must be uploaded to ensure a smooth publication process.
